---
name: playtest-feedback
description: Turn the user's playtest impressions into structured feedback and roadmap changes. Use after the user has played the game and shares observations.
---

1. Ask (briefly) for anything missing: what they did, what they saw, what they expected, which policy/seed.
2. Record each observation as: observation, expectation, suspected cause, proposed change, priority.
3. Separate bugs from design changes from new ideas.
4. Propose roadmap edits and get agreement, then apply them with the `roadmap-update` skill.
5. Log any lasting design choice in `docs/decisions.md`.
